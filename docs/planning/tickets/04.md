## What to build

Complete attributed cash sales with employee discounts and retained paid values. Deliver the complete staff/customer behavior below with the necessary persistence, business rules, API, usable Arabic UI and verification. The approved parent specification is authoritative.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Cashier edits a cart using selected variants, completes a cash sale with optional customer name/phone, and sees the saved original/detail record with employee, authorized branch and occurrence time. Manager sees all sales; employee sees only permitted own records.
- [ ] Validate aggregate quantities across repeated variant lines against known stock. Allocate oldest available batches atomically with the sale/movements/operation receipt. Receipt ten at 100 plus ten at 140 then sell three leaves seven old and ten new.
- [ ] Employee chooses a nonnegative EGP deduction with no manager cap, approval or margin floor and optional reason. Zero final total succeeds; negative fails. Preserve original/discount/final values and applying employee.
- [ ] Use integer piasters and retained equal/capped unit allocation: 300/200 minus 50 gives 275/175; two 300 plus 200 minus 60 gives 280/280/180; 10/100 minus 50 gives 0/60; stable piaster distribution conserves totals.
- [ ] Persist stable sale/operation identities and distinguish physical completion from synchronization; a lost response/retry cannot duplicate a sale or stock deduction. Do not discard an already physically completed sale because a concurrent allocation won; retain a visible discrepancy if necessary.
- [ ] Demonstrate receiving-to-cash-sale-to-authorized-sales-view in the real browser and test quantity/privacy/FIFO/discount/idempotency through real PostgreSQL HTTP operations. Complete offline use is delivered by the dependent tickets.
- [ ] Run the established typecheck, architecture, relevant behavior tests and build gates; preserve existing checks and record verification. No application test is considered passed merely because it is written.
