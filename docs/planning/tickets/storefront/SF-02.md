## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tiles use the returned summary ID/name/preview/availability/lowest price; label starting prices as such and handle null without inventing zero.
- [ ] Near-zero-gutter, flat 4:5 imagery; four wide-screen columns and two phone columns where legible, with accessible narrow/zoom fallback.
- [ ] Out of Stock and Coming Soon stay visible/non-orderable as appropriate; Hidden never appears. Price remains visible without hover.
- [ ] Product links and concise captions work by keyboard/touch; missing/broken images have a stable, accessible fallback. No fabricated second-angle hover image.
- [ ] Reserve image space, prioritize only genuine above-fold lead media and lazy-load the remainder; preserve direct raw catalog URLs and no-store revocation. No optimizer or service-worker media cache.
- [ ] Existing cart and product navigation behavior and hooks remain intact.
