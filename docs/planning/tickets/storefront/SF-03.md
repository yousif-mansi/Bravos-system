- [ ] Hero links to an existing product or category query; no new route, fake seasonal campaign or unsupported claim.

- [ ] Search/category browsing remains reachable. Hero does not dominate filtered/search-result presentation.
- [ ] No carousel, autoplay video, countdown, equal-weight competing heroes or generic sale banner.
- [ ] Absent/invalid optional content yields an honest shop entry, but SF-03 is not accepted as a photography delivery until approved real imagery is demonstrated.
