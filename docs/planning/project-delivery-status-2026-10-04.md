## Five milestones to finish

1. **Freeze a reviewed release candidate.** Engineering reviews the pending QA diff, resolves any demonstrated findings and assembles consistent final-source build, migration, HTTP/browser and offline evidence. Commit only after owner approval. Exit: an identified checkpoint, documented passes/limits and no unclassified pending release changes.

2. **Accept real operating workflows.** Owner supplies an adequate catalog with consistent basic product photos/factual copy, opening quantities/cost batches by branch, staff permissions, prices/offers and delivery/contact configuration. Engineering previews/imports approved data through the supported workflow. Owner/staff exercise sales, payments, outages/reconnect, receiving, orders, Returns, reports and restore on real target devices. Exit: reconciled opening data and signed-off representative workflows; no silent central-stock reassignment.

3. **Accept the final storefront.** Owner approves brand/content and merchandise representation. Engineering completes production desktop/phone visual review, accessibility, physical-device checks and SF-11 matched baseline/final measurements with representative media and interaction traces. Exit: critical SF-11/SF-12 gates closed with evidence and explicit acceptance; any remaining nonblocking limitations documented. No additional campaigns, accounts or payment providers are required.

4. **Prepare hosted operation and training.** After website completion, owner approves hosting/domain choices and supplies operator access. Engineering configures HTTPS, trusted proxy handling, supervised services, database/media persistence, monitoring and recovery, following [release rehearsal](../development/release-rehearsal.md) and [restore guidance](../development/backups/restore.md). Train manager/cashiers on everyday work, sync exceptions and backups. Exit: hosted smoke checks, backup/restore drill and an operator who can run the system.

5. **Pilot internally, then enable the public store.** Owner/staff run an internal pilot, reconcile stock/totals and close demonstrated operational issues. Enable public ordering after delivery and customer-flow acceptance. Exit: stable internal operation, approved public launch, recorded handover and recovery responsibilities. No dates or durations are promised.
