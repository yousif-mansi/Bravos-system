# Operational product stock and a separate warehouse

New merchandise is received into a central warehouse and moved to a retail branch only by an explicit handover transfer. A product has one stock quantity and one selling price at each location. Website size and color choices record what the customer asked for and draw on that shared quantity. Existing branch balances stay on their current batches, as ADR 0005 requires; they are not relocated into the warehouse. The warehouse is not a fulfillment branch and is not added to website availability.
