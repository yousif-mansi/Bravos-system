# Complete physical sales regardless of connectivity

BRAVOS treats a branch cash sale as final when the customer has received the merchandise and paid, even if the branch is offline; the application must preserve the completed sale locally and synchronize it without duplicating or silently discarding its effect. The user chose immediate physical selling with priority over pending online requests, which are checked and confirmed later by a representative through WhatsApp. This requires eventual synchronization of one logical central inventory rather than blocking completed sales on a live server balance; website availability cannot reflect an offline sale before it is communicated, while already-confirmed online items are protected through physical set-aside as recorded in [ADR-0002](0002-protect-confirmed-online-items.md).
